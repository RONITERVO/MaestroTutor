Tell us which device and app version you use, what you tried, and what happened. Share only the details needed to explain the problem. Do not send passwords, API keys or payment-card details.

Account deletion

To delete a Maestro managed account, choose Delete managed account in the app or open the account-deletion page and sign in with that account. If you cannot access it or deletion fails, email the support address above.

Deleting a managed account is separate from removing files on your devices. Uninstalling the app does not delete the managed account. See the privacy policy for retention details.

Local conversations and Quest creations

Conversations, task history and Quest creations are stored on the device where you use them. Use the relevant history, workspace and library controls to remove content. Clearing app data removes that installation's local data; make any backups you want to keep first.

Files you exported, copies in backups and copies on other devices must be removed separately. Deleting Maestro content does not delete the room scan stored by Meta.

Reporting a problem with content

Use the app's AI-response report control for an AI response you want reviewed. For another content concern, email support with a description. Do not post private conversations, room images or account details in public issue reports.
