Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

Maestro Tutor offers two ways to access Google Gemini: bring your own API key ("BYOK") or sign in to use Maestro managed access. The data flow depends on the access mode you choose.

BYOK access

  • API key: A Google AI Studio API key you enter is stored locally on your device. Maestro does not send that key to its managed service.
  • Generation content: Prompts and media are sent directly from your device to Google Gemini.
  • Local data: Chat history and local usage information are stored on your device. Clear local app/browser data to remove it from that installation. Files you export, copies in device backups and copies on other devices are separate.

Managed access

When you choose managed access, Maestro processes the following data to provide, secure, bill, and maintain the service:

  • Account data: Google/Firebase sign-in identifiers and available profile details such as your name, email address, and profile image.
  • Generation content: Prompts, responses, and uploaded media pass through Maestro's Firebase/Google Cloud service and then Google Gemini. Maestro does not store ordinary chat history in its managed database. A one-use Live gateway ticket may hold the Live model setup instructions only until that ticket is consumed or expires; its setup content and secret hash are then removed while non-content operational metadata is retained temporarily. Managed uploads can remain temporarily in Google Gemini Files until deletion or automated cleanup; Maestro stores operational file metadata needed to manage that lifecycle.
  • Usage and operations: Credit balance, entitlements, usage and billing ledger metadata, reservations, rate-limit windows, request identifiers, and file/cleanup state.
  • Purchases: Stripe customer and Checkout references, verification results, and credit-grant records. Stripe processes payment credentials; Maestro does not store raw card details. Stripe Checkout is the only managed-credit purchase provider. Android users can spend a balance purchased on the web; the Android buy action remains unavailable unless Maestro is approved and configured for an applicable Google Play external-checkout programme.
  • Content reports: If you voluntarily report an AI response, Maestro stores the reported content, selected reason, optional notes, surface/model details, and basic request/account metadata so the report can be reviewed.

Maestro Quest and room tasks

Quest v1 is for adults aged 18 or older. Each new book session asks you to confirm you are 18 or older before chat and AI features start. This is self-confirmation, not identity or age verification. Maestro does not request a birth date or save that confirmation as an age record.

  • Your creations: Imported models and animations, drawings, room objects, behaviour programs and their saved settings are stored in the headset's app data. Exported room backups are separate files under your control.
  • Room scans and tracking: With room-access permission, the app loads the Meta room scan to place collision surfaces and support movement. Scanned floor/wall geometry and continuous head/hand/controller tracking are used locally; the room agent does not receive the raw scan mesh or a continuous tracking stream. It can receive bounded room status (such as whether a scan is loaded and surface counts) and the authored objects' positions and settings when relevant to a task.
  • Passthrough and microphone: Viewing passthrough does not upload camera imagery to Gemini. This Quest version does not expose its passthrough camera as a chat camera. Microphone permission is separate, and audio is sent when you use speech features. Camera context in supported Maestro clients is controlled separately.
  • Virtual-room snapshots: When a room task needs to inspect your virtual creations, it can capture an image containing Maestro, your virtual objects and programmable buttons. It excludes the book/chat pages, tool trays, scanned room geometry and real camera or passthrough imagery. That task can send the image through your existing Maestro AI connection and retain the same image in Task details and chat backups. Manual or background captures are not automatically sent to AI. Task copies follow the same history deletion and backup rules as other task evidence.
  • Delegated AI tasks: A room task uses the existing Maestro AI connection and access mode. Its requests can include the source conversation, attachments, language settings and relevant room objects, programs and action results. A task delegated from Live can reuse the audio and enabled camera frames already sent during that turn, both for planning and the final reply. This may include private speech or room images.
  • Task history: The source context, action results and included Live media are retained with the local task journal and can be included in chat-history exports. The journal is pruned when its source history is removed. Deleting history does not remove copies you previously exported or already sent to a provider.
  • Quest managed access: When enabled, Meta entitlement and integrity checks establish that the app is eligible for managed access. Maestro's service verifies a short-lived Meta integrity token and request challenge before issuing Firebase App Check access. It does not retain that token or Meta device identifiers, or request your Meta profile, friends or Meta Avatar. Meta processes platform data under its own policies.
  • Account linking: To link the book to your existing Maestro account, you approve a short-lived code after Google sign-in in your browser. The service holds hashes of the pairing code and device secret, timestamps, status and, while approved, your Maestro account ID. The account ID and secret hash are removed on redemption or cancellation. Hashed IP/account rate-limit subjects help prevent abuse.

Retention

  • Local chat, task journals, imported content and saved rooms remain until you remove them or clear that installation's app data. Exported files and device backups can remain separately.
  • Rate-limit windows are scheduled for deletion two minutes after their window begins.
  • Quest integrity challenges and account-link records expire after five minutes and are scheduled for deletion at expiry. A consumed integrity challenge is deleted immediately. Consumed/cancelled account links retain only timestamps and status until cleanup.
  • Consumed or expired Live ticket metadata, released Live-session metadata, and deleted-file metadata are scheduled for deletion after 30 days. Live ticket setup content and secret hashes are removed when the ticket is consumed or expires.
  • Settled or released reservations and checkout-grant snapshots are scheduled for deletion after 90 days.
  • AI-content reports are scheduled for deletion after 365 days unless account deletion anonymizes them sooner.
  • Managed billing and usage ledgers are kept while the managed account exists. Purchase-claim integrity records are retained to prevent the same external purchase from granting credits twice.

Scheduled deletion is asynchronous, so an expired record may remain briefly before the storage service removes it.

Account deletion

Uninstalling does not delete a managed account, exported files or copies in backups. A managed user can choose Delete managed account in the app or use the account-deletion page. Deletion removes the Firebase-authenticated Maestro account, its managed user data and files, and the linked Stripe customer when applicable, including pending Quest account-link approvals. Local chat and room files, and exported backups, must be removed separately. Purchase-integrity claims and previously submitted content reports may remain only in anonymized form. A one-way account tombstone is retained to prevent delayed requests or payment events from recreating the deleted account. Stripe, Google, and Google Play may retain payment, service, app-distribution, or integrity records under their own policies and legal obligations.

Use care with sensitive information.
Content sent to Gemini is subject to the terms and data practices that apply to your chosen access mode and provider account. Do not include passwords or information you are not authorized to share.

Third-Party Services

Maestro uses Google Gemini, Firebase and Google Cloud, Stripe for managed-credit purchases, and Google Play for Android distribution and Play Integrity App Check—not for managed-credit payments. Quest distribution, entitlement, integrity and room services use Meta. Their handling of data is governed by their own terms and policies. See Google's Privacy Policy, Gemini API Terms, Firebase Privacy and Security, Stripe's Privacy Center, and Meta's Privacy Policy.

Security and policy changes

Maestro uses encrypted network connections and access controls intended to protect managed data. No system can guarantee absolute security. This policy will be updated when the service's material data practices change, with a new effective date shown above.
